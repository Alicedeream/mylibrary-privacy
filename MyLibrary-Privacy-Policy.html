My Library Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Overview

My Library is a local reading, book management, and personal study app. We respect your privacy.

Information We Collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or browsing history. Imported EPUB files, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and answer fields are stored on your device by default.

Network and Third-Party Services

The app is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking. If you voluntarily use Apple system services such as sharing, translation, or the file picker, those services process data under Apple’s privacy terms. We do not sell your books or notes to third parties.

Backup and Export

Backup files are created and saved at your request. If you set a password, the backup is password-protected. You choose where backup files are stored and whether to sync them with iCloud.

User Content and Copyright

You may import EPUB files that you own or are authorized to use. You are responsible for ensuring that imported content does not infringe copyright or other rights. The developer does not provide unauthorized publications.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
